“My College: Before and After” During my college life, I had seven close friends—four girls and three boys. They were all my best friends, and I was always happy and enjoyed spending time with them. But those days did not last forever. I completed my first degree, and after graduation, all my friends started getting jobs. I was happy for them, but I didn’t have a job. At first, I felt sad, but I told myself, “It’s okay. My time will come.” As the days passed, my friends became busy with their jobs, and they stopped calling me as often. Slowly, I started feeling alone. I felt sadness and loneliness, and it became difficult for me. I was tired of feeling this way, so I made an important decision about my life. This is not the end of my story. **This is just the beginning.**
